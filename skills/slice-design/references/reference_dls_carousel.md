## Layout
- Width: 360px (full screen, cards overflow and scroll)
- Scroll: horizontal, cards peek from edges
- Pair with: Dot Indicator for pagination

## Card Types
- Marketing card: 264x70, icon + title + subtitle
- To-do card v2: 312x72, action-oriented

## Pagination indicator — dots, not counter
Carousels use **dots** (animated active pill — 20×6 V-500 on active, 6×6 outline-bold on inactive) as the pagination indicator. Never a "2 / 5" counter chip (which is image-gallery convention, not slice).
