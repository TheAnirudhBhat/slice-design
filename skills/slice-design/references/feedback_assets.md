name: Never substitute user-provided assets
description: Never replace icons, illustrations, or images with your own versions — ask the user when stuck
type: feedback
---
Never replace user-provided assets (icons, illustrations, images) with your own versions.

**Why:** Claude defaults to generating SVGs or substitutes that are always wrong. The user's assets are intentional design choices.

**How to apply:** If an asset isn't loading or is inaccessible, ask the user. Do not silently swap in your own version.
